LLM inference hardware

Each hardware tab puts one capability against its two costs, purchase price and electrical power, first for a GPU, then for a complete computer, and then shows what that capability buys: memory sets the smartest model you can load, bandwidth the generation speed, compute the prefill speed. Higher and further left means more capability for the cost. All points are the same size; color gives the brand and shape the family.

Prices mix MSRPs, street prices seen in October 2026, market or analyst estimates, and supplier quotes converted at $1.12 per €; each tooltip gives the basis. Compute values are the dense 16-bit peaks vendors publish; ≈ marks a value scaled from a sibling chip. GPUs without a known price (H100, H200, MI300X, MI325X, Radeon AI PRO R9700S and R9600, RTX PRO 6000 Server) appear only on the power charts. These hardware tables are a compilation in data/atlas/ without per-row source links; the measurements carry their URL.
