LLM inference hardware

Each hardware tab puts one capability against its two costs, purchase price and electrical power, first for a GPU, then for a complete computer, and then shows what that capability buys. Higher and further left means more capability for the cost. All points are the same size; shape and color give the family. Every value comes from a cited source in the repository's CSV tables.
